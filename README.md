# Retail Supply Chain & ERP Mastery

A free 3-month course for software developers who want to understand retail supply chain, inventory, warehouse (WMS), ERP, order management, demand planning, distribution centers, SAP basics, business analysis, documentation and testing.

### 👉 Open the course: **https://mjnamjkaze.github.io/CodeQuick/**

No account, no login. Your progress stays in your own browser.

---

## What is inside

- **3 months, 13 modules, 45 topics.** Each topic starts with the simple explanation, then the lesson with pictures, real supermarket / retail / DC examples, formulas with numbers, and a developer section.
- **Knowledge check** of 10 questions per topic. You need 9 right (85%) to pass and to count the topic as Mastered.
- **Module mastery check** (20 questions) and **month exams** (30+ questions) with the final exam (40+ questions).
- **Explanations for every answer**, including why each wrong choice is wrong.
- **Flashcards and glossary** for all key terms. Cards come back on a spaced schedule.
- **Review queue**, progress dashboard, bookmarks and history.
- **Capstone**: a replenishment project for a supermarket chain (Supplier → DC → Store).
- Vietnamese text with English terms. Dark and light mode. Works on phones.

Business rules are labelled as industry standard, common practice, or an example from one company. The numbers are examples for practice.
